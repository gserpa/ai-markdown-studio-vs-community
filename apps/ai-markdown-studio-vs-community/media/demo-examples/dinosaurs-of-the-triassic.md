# What Was the Triassic? 🌋

The Triassic period lasted from about **252 to 201 million years ago**.

- The Earth had just one giant continent called **Pangaea** 🗺️
- The climate was **hot and dry** — like a giant desert!
- There were no polar ice caps ❄️ at all
- Dinosaurs were **brand new** — they were just getting started
- They shared the world with giant reptiles and early mammals

> 🤯 Fun fact: If you squished all of today's continents back together, you'd get Pangaea!

<!--notes: Use the Pangaea fact as a mind-blowing moment. Kids love the idea of all continents stuck together like puzzle pieces.-->

<!--slide: image-right-->
# Before the Dinosaurs… 💀

A massive extinction wiped out **96% of all species** just before the Triassic — called the **"Great Dying."**

- Volcanoes erupted for millions of years 🌋
- The oceans turned acidic 🌊
- Most animals disappeared
- This opened the door for **dinosaurs to rise!**

![Triassic landscape](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/30/Pangaea_%28230_million_years_ago%29.png)

<!--notes: Keep it light — frame the extinction as the dinosaurs' big chance, not just doom and gloom. "The bad news made room for the BEST news!"-->

# Eoraptor 🌟

Meet **Eoraptor** — one of the very **first dinosaurs ever!**

- Only about **1 metre long** — the size of a dog 🐕
- Lived in what is now **Argentina** 🇦🇷
- Could run fast on two legs
- Ate plants AND small animals — very greedy! 😋
- Its name means **"dawn stealer"**

![Eoraptor reconstruction](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Eoraptor_lunensis_recon.jpg)

<!--notes: Kids love the dog-size comparison. Ask "Would you want an Eoraptor as a pet?" to spark engagement.-->

---

<!--slide: image-right-->
# Herrerasaurus 💪

**Herrerasaurus** was a fierce predator of the early Triassic!

- About **6 metres long** — bigger than a car 🚗
- Super fast runner on two strong legs
- Had **sharp, curved teeth** for catching prey 🦷
- Also found in **Argentina**
- One of the largest hunters of its time

![Herrerasaurus reconstruction](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Herrerasaurus_reconstruction.jpg)

<!--notes: Compare size to a car for instant impact. Ask kids to imagine a 6-metre-long lizard running at them!-->

---

<!--slide: image-right-->
# Plateosaurus 🌿

**Plateosaurus** was one of the first BIG dinosaurs!

- Up to **10 metres long** 📏
- Ate plants — a gentle giant 🍃
- Walked on two legs but could lean down on four
- Lived in what is now **Germany and Switzerland** 🇩🇪
- Travelled in herds for safety

![Plateosaurus skeleton](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/77/Plateosaurus_engelhardti.png)

<!--notes: Emphasise that even big herbivores were around early — not all early dinos were scary predators. Great moment to show variety.-->

---

<!--slide: image-right-->
# Coelophysis 🏃

**Coelophysis** was a speedy little hunter!

- About **3 metres long** and very lightweight
- Hollow bones made it super light — that's what its name means! 🦴
- Lived in packs, like wolves 🐺
- Found in **New Mexico, USA** 🇺🇸
- One of the **most well-known** Triassic dinosaurs

![Coelophysis](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/58/Coelophysis_skeleton.jpg)

<!--notes: The hollow-bone fact is a great hook — link it forward to birds having hollow bones too. "Coelophysis is a distant cousin of your budgie!"-->

# Plant-Eaters vs Meat-Eaters 🥦🥩

## 🥦 Plant-Eaters
- Long necks to reach leaves high up
- Flat, wide teeth for grinding plants
- Big round bodies for digesting tough plants
- Often travelled in herds
- **Examples:** Plateosaurus, Riojasaurus

## 🥩 Meat-Eaters
- Sharp, curved teeth for gripping prey
- Fast legs for chasing animals
- Lighter bodies for quick movement
- Often hunted alone or in small packs
- **Examples:** Eoraptor, Herrerasaurus, Coelophysis

<!--notes: This is a perfect compare-and-contrast moment. Ask kids to guess which type they'd rather be. Encourage debate!-->

# Not Everything Was a Dinosaur! 😲

The Triassic world was full of other amazing creatures too!

- 🐊 **Aetosaurs** — armoured plant-eating reptiles that looked like tanks
- 🦈 **Ichthyosaurs** — dolphin-shaped reptiles that ruled the seas
- 🦅 **Pterosaurs** — the first flying reptiles (NOT dinosaurs!)
- 🐾 **Cynodonts** — small furry animals that were early relatives of **you!**
- 🐢 **Early turtles** — turtles have barely changed in 220 million years!

> 🐢 Turtles today look almost exactly the same as Triassic turtles. They are living fossils!

<!--notes: Kids are often surprised that pterosaurs aren't dinosaurs. Use this as a fun myth-busting moment.-->

# What Did the World Look Like? 🌍

The Triassic world looked very different from today!

- No grass anywhere 🌾 — it hadn't evolved yet!
- Giant **fern forests** covered the land 🌿
- Huge **conifer trees** (like giant Christmas trees 🎄) dominated
- **Red and orange deserts** in the centre of Pangaea
- Shallow warm seas around the edges 🌊

![Pangaea map](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Pangaea_continents.png)

<!--notes: Ask kids to close their eyes and imagine walking through a fern forest the size of a country. No flowers, no grass, just giant ferns!-->

# How Do We Know All This? 🔍

Scientists called **palaeontologists** dig up clues called **fossils!**

- 🦴 **Bone fossils** — the actual bones turned to stone over millions of years
- 👣 **Footprint fossils** — dinosaur tracks preserved in ancient mud
- 🥚 **Egg fossils** — some Triassic dinosaur eggs have been found!
- 🌿 **Plant fossils** — leaves and wood turned to stone too
- 💩 **Coprolites** — fossilised dinosaur poop! (Yes, really! 😄)

> 🔬 Palaeontologists are like time-travelling detectives — they solve mysteries that are 200 million years old!

<!--notes: The coprolite fact always gets a laugh from 10-year-olds. Lean into it — it makes science feel fun and accessible.-->

<!--slide: image-right-->
# Finding Fossils 🪨

Some of the best Triassic fossils come from amazing places around the world!

- 🇦🇷 **Ischigualasto, Argentina** — "Valley of the Moon," one of the richest Triassic fossil sites
- 🇩🇪 **Germany** — home to Plateosaurus fossils
- 🇺🇸 **New Mexico, USA** — thousands of Coelophysis fossils found together
- 🇿🇦 **South Africa** — early dinosaur relatives found here

![Ischigualasto Valley](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/67/Ischigualasto_NT.jpg)

<!--notes: Point out that Argentina is a huge hotspot — ask kids if any of them have ever been to South America. Make it feel like an adventure destination.-->

# The Triassic-Jurassic Extinction 🌋💥

About **201 million years ago**, another big extinction hit!

- Massive volcanic eruptions split **Pangaea** apart 🌋
- Lava covered areas the size of entire continents
- Many animals disappeared — including most big reptiles 😢
- But most **dinosaurs survived!** 🦕✅
- This gave dinosaurs the chance to grow even **bigger and more diverse**
- The Jurassic period — age of the giants — was about to begin! 🦖

> 🌟 Surviving the extinction was dinosaurs' greatest superpower. The Jurassic was their golden age!

# Triassic vs Jurassic Dinosaurs ⚔️

## 🦕 Triassic
- Small to medium size
- Just getting started
- Shared the world with many other reptiles
- Fast and agile hunters
- World was one big continent

## 🦖 Jurassic
- Giants appeared — massive sauropods!
- Dinosaurs totally dominated the land
- T. Rex's ancestors evolved
- Continents began to split apart
- Lush forests replaced dry deserts
